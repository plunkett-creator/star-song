Star Song: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Star Song (the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, and this website) does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The pieces run entirely on your device. The apps work offline.
  • Each piece remembers a few settings (such as pace or number of voices) on your device, so it opens the way you left it. These never leave your device.
  • Purchases in the apps are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only learn from the store whether the collection has been unlocked on your device.

If you have any questions, contact you@example.com.
